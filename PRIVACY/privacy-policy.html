Privacy Policy for SCIENOVA AI

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Welcome to SCIENOVA AI. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the SCIENOVA AI platform.

1. Information We Collect

SCIENOVA AI is designed to minimize data collection to ensure a safe learning environment:

  • User Inputs & Queries: Science-related questions and class level selections entered into the chat interface to generate accurate learning responses.
  • Local Memory & History: Saved chat history and preferences stored locally on your device (via browser LocalStorage) for revision purposes.
  • Technical Data: Basic device type and browser information used purely for UI responsiveness and bug fixing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Providing customized educational content tailored to your selected class level (Class 6th to NEET).
  • Improving the performance and accuracy of the SCIENOVA AI Brain Engine.
  • Maintaining your local search history for easy access during study sessions.

3. Children's Privacy

SCIENOVA AI is an educational tool suitable for students. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information (PII) like full names, addresses, or phone numbers from children.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your chat interactions and study history are primarily stored locally on your own device. We implement industry-standard encryption practices to ensure your interactions with the app remain secure.

5. Third-Party Services

SCIENOVA AI does not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to third parties. If third-party APIs are used for backend processing, data is processed anonymously without attached personal identities.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
